About Me

Hello! I'm Naman Pal, an Engineering Specialist at Canada Life, where I help build and maintain reliable, scalable enterprise systems for a leading Canadian organization whose products and services reach over 1 in 3 Canadians.

I like to explore new technologies and build projects focused on solving real-world problems.

I am fascinated by how much progress we, the people of Earth, have made in the past few decades, whether it's the rapid advancement of medical science, the dramatic reduction in global poverty, or the growing accessibility of knowledge to everyone. The list goes on.

While these are incredible accomplishments, they represent only a teeny-tiny fraction of what we, the people, are capable of! It would be an understatement to say there's still so much left to do. Through my work, I plan on speeding up our progress and helping humanity move forward at lightspeed in every way possible.

I envision a world where everyone has ample opportunity to contribute to our collective success. That starts with meeting everyone's basic needs, ensuring that people know we've got each other's backs, inspiring them to recognize what they're capable of achieving, and removing the barriers in their way so they can actually make their invaluable contributions.

Sounds like a dream, right? Let's turn it into reality!

Let's solve the smaller problems quickly so we can tackle the bigger ones, eventually expanding beyond our galaxy cluster and figuring out what this universe is really about. What's the point of it all? Why did God—or some higher power, if one exists—create all of this? And if there is no higher power, how did we get here in the first place, and... what's next?
